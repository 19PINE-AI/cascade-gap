## 2. Two papers to add to §2

**A. The Cascade Equivalence Hypothesis (arXiv 2602.17598, Feb 2026).** Argues speech LLMs behave like ASR→LLM cascades *internally* — uses logit lens + LEACE to show "literal transcripts emerge from hidden states" and "text representations are causally necessary." Shows cascade advantages widen under noise (up to 7.6% at 0 dB). This is a gift for our mechanism story (§3 Move 3): if speech LLMs are already internally cascading, our externalized cascade is making the implicit explicit, and the augmented-cascade conditions are loading the bottleneck with content the internal cascade can't carry.

**B. MMAR (arXiv 2505.13032, NeurIPS 2025 D&B).** 1,000 audio-QA triplets stratified across four reasoning layers: Signal → Perception → Semantic → Cultural. This stratification *is* the symbolic→perceptual axis the paper proposes. Use MMAR as a first-class audio task, not just a baseline.

**C. MMAU-Pro (arXiv 2508.13992, Aug 2025)** adds long-form stratification (≤30s / 30s-3min / 3-8min / 8-10min). Good for a latency/context-length side study.

## 3. Scooping risk assessment

The two nearest neighbors:

**Beyond Transcription (2604.12506, 2026-04-14).** Proposes a Unified Audio Schema (transcription + paralinguistics + non-linguistic events) — identical in spirit to our C2 augmented cascade. But they do this as a **training-time supervised objective** on 13,500 hours of UAS-labeled data, tested only on audio (MMSU, MMAR, MMAU). The paper does NOT:
- Run same-weights inference-time prompting on unmodified models.
- Cross-modalities.
- Propose a task-property predictor.

**Our differentiators stand.** But two defensive moves are warranted:
1. **Explicit comparison table in §2** that lists, for each antecedent, (a) training-time vs inference-time, (b) single-modality vs cross-modality, (c) same-weights protocol or not, (d) predictive rule or not. Beyond Transcription will differ on all four.
2. **Run a side experiment on MMSU** that compares our C2 (prompted, unmodified model) against a UAS-trained baseline from Beyond Transcription, if they release weights. This directly answers "why not just train for UAS?"

**Cascade Equivalence Hypothesis (2602.17598).** Audio-only, mechanistic, no cross-modality claim, no predictive rule. Complementary. Cite in §2 Silo 1 *and* in §3 Move 3 mechanism story.

**No paper found that does cross-modality same-weights cascade comparison with a task-property predictor.** Our core novelty claim survives Phase 0 scrutiny.

## 7. Risks surfaced during Phase 0 that aren't in §5

- **Closed-source rate limits on audio.** Audio-preview tiers have tighter rate limits than text tiers. A 18-cell × 5-task × 3-condition × 3-seed matrix on GPT-4o-audio alone is ~800 audio calls; at preview rate limits could take days to a week per model. Build the harness with persistent caching from day 1 — re-running is the norm.
- **MMAR leaking into training sets.** NeurIPS 2025 D&B papers often appear in post-Nov 2025 training data. Gemini 3.1 Pro (Feb 2026 release) may have seen MMAR. Consider a held-out private split, or use MMAR's 2026 extensions if they exist.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Live's audio-native path is separate from standard API.** The "thinking" question (§4.4 side study) gets more complicated for audio, because reasoning-mode on audio is inconsistent across vendors. Keep the side study text-only or document the confound.
